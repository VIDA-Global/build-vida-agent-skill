# Optional Discovery

Read this reference only when the user wants help selecting a software ecosystem, ICP, target segment, or workflow. Skip it for a direct build.

## Discovery objective

Select one opportunity where a Vida Agent can deliver a concrete, repeatable business outcome. Market size alone is insufficient.

Evaluate:

- fit with the user's ICP or stated buyer;
- recurring queue/workflow frequency;
- economic consequence of delay or failure;
- bounded decisions and clear exception handling;
- browser/API accessibility and evidence quality;
- practical trial, demo, or paid account access;
- credible ability to seed synthetic data;
- number and quality of target customers;
- differentiation from the target software's built-in automation;
- ability to demonstrate and verify a destination effect.

## BuiltWith branch

Use BuiltWith only when it answers a relevant selection or targeting question. Ask for its credential through the secure access preflight if it is not already available.

For a technology cohort:

1. preserve the exact deterministic query;
2. capture current-site count, geography, spend/traffic or other relevant filters, and recency when available;
3. distinguish website detections from verified unique companies;
4. deduplicate redirects, parent brands, agencies, and obvious non-customer sites when producing a target list;
5. label heuristic classifications and independently verify high-value prospects before outreach;
6. save query date and pagination completeness.

Do not treat BuiltWith counts as revenue, active subscriptions, ICP qualification, or permission to contact.

## Workflow selection

Research the target software's native features before proposing the Agent. Avoid pitching a generic reminder, notification, or report the product already performs adequately.

Prefer a wedge that combines several of:

- selects records by business priority;
- reaches or coordinates with a person;
- captures an unstructured reason or decision;
- applies policy and escalates exceptions;
- writes a structured result into the source system;
- safely retries or deduplicates;
- crosses browser software that lacks a suitable public integration.

## Decision artifact

Return a short opportunity brief containing:

- target software and buyer;
- evidence-backed market signal;
- one recommended workflow and measurable outcome;
- existing native automation and why the proposal differs;
- account-access and synthetic-data feasibility;
- likely helper/Agent/channel shape;
- major risks and required approvals;
- recommendation to build, investigate further, or reject.

Select one opportunity before entering the construction workflow. Do not broaden the build to multiple software ecosystems unless the user explicitly asks for a portfolio comparison or batch.
